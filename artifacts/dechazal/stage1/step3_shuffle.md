# Step 3: within-record shuffle of the fold models' inputs

Each held-out fold is re-scored by its own INT8 fold model after shuffling the R-R vectors (`rr`), the spectrograms (`spec`) or both (independently) among beats of the same record. Values are averaged over shuffle seeds 0 1 2 within a seed, then mean ± std over model seeds 42 43 44. `delta_*` = shuffled - unshuffled. The unshuffled scores were checked to reproduce oof_predictions.npz exactly.
